# Vanguard Academy

A Godot 4 game. You carry a sword and a spell in every fight. Most of the school day is hunting, upgrading, and walking between places. The Dueling Court and the Proving Hall are the two places that switch to the battle theme.

## Try it

Godot 4.7 is installed on this computer at:

`C:\Users\User\AppData\Local\Godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe`

Open this folder as a project and press F5. Or from a terminal:

```
& "C:\Users\User\AppData\Local\Godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe" --path "C:\Users\User\Documents\Unity ai game\Magicandswordupgradegame4"
```

Drag the window tall, square, or wide. The stick and buttons stay large. The yearbook also has phone, iPad, and computer window sizes.

## Play

- Move with the stick, or WASD / arrow keys.
- Cut, Spark, and Step are the big buttons. Keys: J, K, Space.
- The place name opens the place board.
- Rank, in the Yard, opens the forge. Ore comes from the grounds.
- Classroom stamps Spellcut. The library book then teaches Hop Cut.
- Court: practice Even or Open, or host / join a friend on port 7777.
- If Windows Firewall asks when you host, allow the game on private networks.

Between Bells plays in the school places. Ready at the Ring plays in the Court and the Hall.
